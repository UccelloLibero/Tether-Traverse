Tether Traverse

Navigate Mount Rainier terrain with your climbing partner!

How to Play

Mobile

Movement: Hold ← / → (left thumb)

Jump: Tap ↑ (right thumb)

Pause / Sound: Buttons top-right

Both climbers move together. Play in landscape!

Desktop

Player 1: ← → to move, ↑ to jump

Player 2: A / D to move, W to jump

Pause: Esc  ·  Sound: M

Control both climbers simultaneously!

Goal: Reach the summit at 14,410 ft, then make it home safely.

Rope: Mind the slack! Keep climbers 1–3 m apart. Spacing is scored at the summit.

Supplies: 3 L water, 3,000 cal. Rest stops use some; keep 0.5 L + 250 cal for the descent.
